# Future Implementations
## Do
- Capture the gap, why it matters, and the next concrete step.
- Keep each item small and specific.
- Mark items done when the implementation or test lands.
## Do not
- Do not bury open work in vague prose.
- Do not mix planning with finished work.
## Verify
- Make sure each unresolved item has a follow-up task.
