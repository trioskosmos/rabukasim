# Unified Card Search
## Do
- Search by card number, name, and known variants.
- Record the source location and report-ready evidence.
- Reconcile mismatched names or IDs before writing conclusions.
## Do not
- Do not rely on a single search hit.
- Do not publish an ID until the source is confirmed.
## Verify
- Confirm the card appears in the source data and the report output.
