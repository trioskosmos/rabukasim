# Frontend Sync
## Do
- Sync from the master asset source only.
- Treat the delivery folder as generated output.
- Verify the copied assets match the intended source.
## Do not
- Do not edit the delivery folder by hand.
- Do not sync from an outdated mirror.
## Verify
- Refresh the app or launcher and confirm the new assets appear.
