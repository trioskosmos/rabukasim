# Ability Verification
## Do
- Identify the exact card, trigger, and effect chain.
- Compare source text, frame program, and runtime output.
- Isolate one discrepancy at a time.
## Do not
- Do not mix multiple cards or rules in one pass.
- Do not let commentary replace a real engine check.
## Verify
- Capture the mismatch and the corrected trace in the same run.
