KEY STRUCTURAL PATTERNS IDENTIFIED
================================================================================

1. Primary break point: ： (colon) - separates cost from effect
2. Secondary break points: 。 (period) - separates action sequences
3. Tertiary break points: 、 (comma) - separates OR conditions, concurrent actions
4. Condition markers: 場合、 時 かぎり につき
5. Optional markers: てもよい まで か、 (OR)
6. Choice markers: 選ぶ 代わりに 以下から1つを選ぶ
7. Follow-up markers: そうした場合 さらに その後
8. Parenthetical rules: （...） - clarification/exceptions

PROPOSED TEMPLATE STRUCTURE
================================================================================
[TRIGGER] → [COST?] ： [CONDITION?] → [ACTION SEQUENCE] → [OPTIONAL FOLLOW-UP]
